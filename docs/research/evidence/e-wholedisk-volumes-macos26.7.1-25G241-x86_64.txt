FINDINGS
1. A whole-disk file system has no Partitions and no APFSVolumes in `list`; its VolumeName (and MountPoint when mounted) sit on the disk entry; Content is "".
2. `info` on such a disk names FilesystemType (msdos, hfs) and VolumeUUID. Measured unmounted: hfs only (FAT was measured mounted, exFAT/NTFS not at all); MountPoint is "" when unmounted.
3. An APFS -layout NONE image: the store disk12 has Content "", no FilesystemType, APFSContainerReference disk13; the container disk13 lists APFSPhysicalStores [disk12] (a whole disk, not a partition).
4. Before the fix, VolumeDiscovery.enumerate did not list disk10/disk11, and DiskTopology attached container disk13 to no disk.
5. A blank whole disk (disk8/disk9, the leftovers above): `list` entry {Content: "", no VolumeName, no Partitions}; `info`
   FilesystemType, FilesystemName and VolumeUUID absent, VolumeName "", MountPoint "". Fixture diskutil-info-wholedisk-blank.plist.
NOT MEASURED: an unmounted exFAT/NTFS/FAT whole disk; CoreStorage; `apfs addVolume` / `eraseVolume` on a container whose store is a
whole disk (H17 measured them with a partition map only).
