# Whole-disk follow-ups: the three cases the 2026-10-18 evidence left NOT MEASURED — 2026-10-26
# Host: macOS 26.7.1 (25G241), x86_64. Disk images only, created under /private/tmp/xcv-wd2; every device acted on was
# attached by the run and re-checked as BusProtocol "Disk Image" before any unmount, erase or add. All detached and deleted
# afterwards. Companion to e-wholedisk-volumes-macos26.7.1-25G241-x86_64.txt.

FINDINGS
1. An UNMOUNTED exFAT whole disk (hdiutil -layout NONE -fs ExFAT): `info` keeps FilesystemType "exfat", FilesystemName,
   VolumeName and VolumeUUID; only MountPoint becomes "". `list` keeps VolumeName on the disk entry. So DiskTopology keeps
   it as a whole-disk volume typed `exfat`, not `unknown`. (FAT32 unmounted: not measured — `hdiutil create` failed
   "Resource busy" right after the previous create; NTFS: macOS cannot write one.)
2. `diskutil apfs addVolume <container> APFS <name> [-quota 1g]` and `diskutil eraseVolume APFS <name> <volume>` — the
   product's exact argv shapes (DiskPreparation.arguments) — both SUCCEED on a container whose physical store is the whole
   disk (2 GB image, -layout NONE): disk8s2 and disk8s3 created, disk8s3 erased and renamed. So the two R6 options the
   2026-10-18 change newly offers on such a drive behave as H17 measured them on a partition map.
3. `eraseVolume` on the whole-disk STORE itself is refused by diskutil: "The target disk is in use by APFS as a Physical
   Store; use diskutil apfs deleteContainer". The product never offers that (eraseVolume targets are partitions and
   role-less APFS volumes; a whole-disk volume is refused for eraseVolume since 2026-10-18).
4. A trap worth recording: on small images, `apfs addVolume` fails "-69493: You can't add any more APFS Volumes to its
   APFS Container". It is a volume ceiling that depends on the container's size (only 256 MB -> one volume and 2 GB -> at least
   three were measured), NOT the whole-disk store: the control — a 256 MB APFS container on a GPT map — fails identically, and a 2 GB whole-disk container takes
   three volumes. A real drive is far above this; a test fixture of 256 MB is not.
5. CoreStorage: `diskutil cs` on macOS 26 lists only list/info/delete/unlockVolume — it can no longer create a logical
   volume group, and `gpt create`/`gpt add` on an attached image fail "operation not permitted". Measured instead: a GPT
   image whose partition 1 type GUID was rewritten in the file (both tables, CRCs fixed) to Apple_CoreStorage
   53746F72-6167-11AA-AA11-00306543ECAC before attaching. `diskutil list` shows it as Content "Apple_CoreStorage"; `info`
   gives no FilesystemType, VolumeName or VolumeUUID; `cs list` finds no group (the partition holds no CS metadata). What
   this establishes: a CoreStorage physical volume reaches DiskTopology as a plain partition whose content is
   "Apple_CoreStorage" and with nothing that says what it holds — which is exactly how the old encrypted Time Machine
   layout (CoreStorage over HFS+) looks from outside. NOT measured: a real CoreStorage group (cannot be made here).

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CODE (2026-10-26, branch fix/wholedisk-followups)
- Before: `DriveEvaluation.options` offered `eraseVolume` on an Apple_CoreStorage partition and `eraseDisk` on its disk;
  `DiskSafety.unverifiedHFSPartitions` counted only HFS+. Now a partition typed Apple_CoreStorage, and an unmounted
  whole-disk volume typed `unknown`, are unverified like an unmounted HFS+ one: `mightBeTimeMachine` refuses erasing it
  and erasing its disk.
