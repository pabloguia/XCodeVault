    (evidence/e11-import-AppleTVOSSimulatorRuntime_Cryptex-…txt:2) but never re-exported,
    and that runtime is now uninstalled — it is the one whose abandoned dyld cache F10

CAVEAT CARRIED INTO THE FINDING
  The two `sizeBytes` are different measurements — logical size for the .dmg,
  allocated bytes for the bundle. Not comparable, and the same field gates
  `sizeBytes > 500_000_000` in installer(for:in:).
